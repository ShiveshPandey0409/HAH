# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Brands and independent creators who need real people to distribute marketing work, and humans who want to earn from public Reddit or LinkedIn accounts they already own. Both groups are moving quickly and need the next useful action to be obvious.

## Product Purpose

HAH turns a marketing brief into paid, trackable human work. Creators publish and review bounties; humans connect a public profile, find eligible work, claim it, submit proof, and get approved. The hackathon product succeeds when that complete loop is easy to demonstrate without explanation.

## Brand Personality

Fast, human, direct. The product should feel energetic and trustworthy without becoming corporate or overly polished.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic purple SaaS dashboards, crypto-finance styling, glassmorphism, marketing fluff, and cute illustrations that distract from the work.

## Design Principles

- Put the next action first.
- Show real work and real status, not vanity metrics.
- Keep creator and human journeys distinct but visually related.
- Make dense setup feel short through progressive disclosure.
- Use plain, compact language throughout.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal compliance target for the hackathon. Preserve inexpensive browser-native basics: semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, and reduced-motion support.
